About Our Project

Our project is a Random Quote Generator, a single-page web app that fetches quotes from the DummyJSON Quotes API. Its main goal is to show how JavaScript's fetch() and async/await can retrieve JSON data from a public API, update the page dynamically, and handle errors gracefully. It also includes category filters, copy and share, a favorites list, and light and dark themes.
